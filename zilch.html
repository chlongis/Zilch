Why here, why now

Minnesota is the best place in America to do this

In 2024 Uber and Lyft threatened to leave Minneapolis over driver pay. The state passed a floor anyway and they stayed. What the fight left behind is a market where drivers know their worth and the rules are already written down.

Minnesota statutory requirements every rideshare network must meet, us included.
