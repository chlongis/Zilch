How it works

Three steps, then it's yours

STEP 01

Build it once

Your trips, your rating, your car, your area. Attach a screenshot of your stats screen and the profile shows as verified. Takes about two minutes.

STEP 02

Hand it out

A permanent link, a QR code for the seatback, a printable card, or save straight into a rider's contacts. However you want to give it out, it's there.

STEP 03

Get found directly

Riders look you up and reach you themselves. You set the price, you keep all of it, and nobody takes a percentage for making the introduction.

Why this matters

Your reputation is the product they're selling

Uber's fee isn't payment for an app. It's payment for being the only one who knows you're good at this.

It doesn't transfer

Four years of five-star ratings is worth nothing the moment you open a different app. That isn't a technical limitation — it's the entire retention strategy.

It vanishes on deactivation

An algorithm decides, there's often no appeal, and everything you built is gone with it. A record you hold a copy of can't be switched off.

It costs you 30% forever

As long as they're the only place your history exists, the cut is whatever they say it is. A rider who can find you directly changes that number to zero.
