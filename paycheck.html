This is an estimate, not a legal determination. The per-trip floor applies to each trip individually, and this can only approximate that from your totals — so the real minimum may be higher if many of your trips were short. In Washington and New York City the whole standard is per trip, which makes this a rougher guide there. A shortfall here means go look at your statements, not that it's proven.
